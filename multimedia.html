1. KGF Chapter 2

KGF stands for Kolar Gold Fields, a historic gold-mining region in Karnataka, India, and is also the title of a hit Kannada action film franchise directed by Prashanth Neel and starring Yash as Rocky. KGF: Chapter 1 (2018) follows Rocky as he rises from poverty in Bombay to infiltrate a brutal, syndicate-run gold mine in KGF to assassinate its ruler, Garuda, eventually rallying the enslaved workers to his side before the story continues in KGF: Chapter 2. In real life, the Kolar Gold Fields operated for over a century—known for deep mining shafts and early power generation—until operations closed in February 2001 due to falling gold prices
